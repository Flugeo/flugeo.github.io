Privacy Policy for the Flugeo app

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Controller: Kevin Eobaldt, Am Festungsgraben 6, 21079 Hamburg, Germany, flugeo@yahoo.com

Flugeo collects no data about you. What you enter stays with you.

1. In short. Flugeo has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer.

2. Your data. Flugeo stores flights, people with photos, recordings and settings on your device. If you back up your device (to iCloud or a computer), they are included. You choose photos yourself; Flugeo only receives the chosen image and has no access to your photo library.

3. Sync with iCloud. If you turn on “Sync with iCloud” in Profile (off by default, on each device separately), Flugeo stores flights, people with photos, recordings, trip titles, trip splits, side trips and the list of deleted imports in your private iCloud database with Apple and syncs them with your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Before flights, people, photos, tracks, notes and trip settings go to your private iCloud database, your device encrypts them. If you’ve turned on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud in Settings, only your devices have the keys; otherwise Apple keeps the keys so it can help you recover your data. Random identifiers, a version number, which entries belong together and what iCloud needs to manage them, such as an entry’s type, size, checksums and when it was changed, are not encrypted. Even with Advanced Data Protection, this information stays under iCloud’s standard data protection, but it contains no content such as places, names or notes. Color theme, units, reminders and the Live Map stay on each device. Flugeo has no server and no access to this database; the developer sees none of it. So that changes also arrive in the background, Apple sends the app a silent notification; Flugeo does not use your location for this. If you turn sync off, the data stays on this device and in iCloud. If you sign out of iCloud, Flugeo deletes nothing: the data stays in iCloud, and Flugeo leaves it on this device. If the system removes the synced data from the device anyway, it comes back as soon as you sign in again with the same Apple Account. To remove it, delete Flugeo.

4. Location. Flugeo asks for your location only when you open the Live Map, and only while you are using the app. It is used only for the Live Map, the display on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island and, if you like, for recording your flight. Flugeo uses your location in the background only while a Live Display is running, and your device shows this. Your position stays on your device. Recordings are stored on your device and in your backups, and with sync turned on also in your private iCloud database. You can change or revoke access at any time under Settings › Apps › Flugeo › Location.

5. Backups. “Save Backup” creates a file that you store or share yourself. If you turn on “Back Up Daily to iCloud Drive” (off by default), Flugeo saves a backup to the “Flugeo” folder in your iCloud Drive right away and then once a day when something has changed. It contains all flights, people with photos, recordings and settings. It is stored in your iCloud account with Apple; the developer cannot read it. Flugeo deletes older automatic backups itself and keeps the most recent ones. If you turn the switch off, no new backups are added; existing ones stay until you delete them. When you turn on sync and, while it’s on, before big changes such as “Delete All Flights”, Flugeo automatically backs up the current state on the device, keeping the last three for each reason, and with automatic backup also in the “Flugeo” folder in iCloud Drive. If Flugeo notices while sync is on that you signed out of iCloud, it backs up as well, but only on the device. If you leave Flugeo while sync is on and changes aren’t in iCloud yet, it backs up that state as well, but only on the device, keeping only the latest of these backups. You’ll find them under “Load Backup”.

6. Import and export. Flugeo reads CSV and calendar files and backups only when you choose them. You share exports such as CSV, GPX or images yourself, using the share sheet.

7. Widgets and Live Activity. Widgets and the Live Activity show your flights on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. For this, Flugeo keeps a selection of your flights and numbers in a folder on your device that the app and its widgets share.

8. Reminders and Siri. Flugeo schedules reminders before planned flights as notifications on your device, without a server. You can turn them off in Profile or under Settings › Apps › Flugeo › Notifications. Flugeo works out answers for Siri and Shortcuts on your device.

9. Map. The world map is built into the app. Flugeo doesn’t download maps from the internet and doesn’t send positions to map services.

10. Crash reports. If Flugeo crashes or stops responding for a while, your device gives the app a report. It contains technical details such as the app and system version, device model, region and the place in the code. Flugeo stores it on your device and never sends it anywhere; like all of the app’s data, it is included in a backup of your device. Under Good to Know › Crash Reports you can see the reports and share them yourself, for example for a bug report. Flugeo keeps at most 30, none longer than 90 days.

11. Reports to Apple. Separately from Flugeo: if “Share With App Developers” is turned on under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on your device, Apple shares its own crash reports and usage statistics with the developer. This is a setting on your device, not in Flugeo; you can turn it off there at any time.

12. Deleting your data. When you delete Flugeo, your device deletes all of the app’s data. To delete backups in iCloud Drive, open the “Flugeo” folder in the Files app. What sync has stored in your iCloud stays there.

13. Contact. For privacy questions, write to flugeo@yahoo.com. Because the app sends no data to me, I cannot hand out, correct or delete any data about you; it is all with you. If you write to me, I use your message only to reply and delete it once the matter is settled; a crash report you send me is used only to find the bug. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your message is stored with my email provider, Yahoo International Limited, Ireland (Yahoo Mail).

14. Your rights. Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

15. Changes. If anything here changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app under “Good to Know” › Privacy Policy.

P. Your purchase. You buy Flugeo on the App Store. Apple handles the purchase. Apple gives me only aggregated sales figures, nothing about you.

W. This website. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time and your browser, also to protect the page against abuse. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement. I set no cookies, use no analytics and embed no content from other providers.
